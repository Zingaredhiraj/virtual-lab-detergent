Experiment Steps

1

Neutralization

LABSA reacts with Na₂CO₃ to form the detergent base (sodium salt).

2

Mixing

Thorough blending of the neutralized slurry to ensure uniform consistency.

3

Additives

Builders, enzymes, fragrances, and optical brighteners are added.

4

Drying

Spray drying converts the slurry into free-flowing powder granules.

5

Packaging

The finished detergent powder is weighed and sealed in packaging.
